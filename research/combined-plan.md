# Combined plan: both papers

*Set 2026-11-05. Holden: FIU sophomore, transferring fall 2027. Goal: credibility,
network, recognition. Both papers valued equally.*

## At a glance

| | **Paper 1: GLP-1s and health care use** | **Paper 2: trades business value** |
|---|---|---|
| **Question** | When people start a GLP-1, do they use more medical care (visits, lab tests, prescriptions, spending), and does that change over time? | What is a trades business worth, and why do so many never sell? |
| **The puzzle** | More care at first (prescriber, check-ins, blood work), maybe less later if health improves. Do costs rise or fall? | Buyers pay far more for shops with recurring revenue that run without the owner. Most shops have neither, so they sell cheaply or close. |
| **Economics** | Health as capital (Grossman); complements vs. substitutes in medical care | Asset valuation; information problems in small-business sales; why roll-ups profit (buy low multiple, sell high) |
| **Data** | Medical Expenditure Panel Survey (free, public, through 2024), via IPUMS | National business-for-sale listings, collected by hand (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) |
| **Method** | Same people before vs. after starting, vs. similar non-starters (difference-in-differences) | Compare asking multiples across features; regression of multiple on recurring revenue, owner role, size, trade, region |
| **Companion piece** | Memo: who wins and loses in the GLP-1 economy (supplements, bars, testing, snacks) | Owner memo: what raises what your trades business is worth; optional online tool |
| **Shows** | Research skill, health economics | Strategy, operations, business economics |
| **Who cares** | Insurers, employers, health systems, health tech, policymakers | Owners, brokers, buyers, PE firms, operators |
| **Repeatable line** | "GLP-1 starters had X more doctor visits in year one" | "Trades shops with recurring revenue ask X times more" |
| **Main limits** | Few new starters per panel; self-reported events; "linked to," not "caused by" | Asking ≠ sale prices; only listed businesses appear; "linked to" |
| **Professor** | Health economist (feedback in Jan) | Finance or entrepreneurship professor (summer/fall) |
| **Folder** | `glp1-health-care-use/`, `glp1-economy-memo/` | `trades-business-value/` |

## Hardest parts
- **Paper 1:** linking MEPS files; small numbers of new starters.
- **Paper 2:** consistent rules for coding messy listing descriptions; enough
  listings with real financials.

## How they help each other
- **Same core skill:** cleaning data, comparing groups, regression. Paper 1 teaches
  it over break; paper 2 reuses it in spring.
- **Two sides of you:** research credibility (paper 1) and business judgment
  (paper 2). Each companion memo turns economics into a recommendation.
- **One idea underneath:** why people and businesses under-invest in things that
  pay off later (their health; systems and recurring revenue).
