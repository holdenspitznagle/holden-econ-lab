# How paper 1 gets made (health care use)

*Set 2026-11-05. ~5–8 hours a week, Nov–Feb. Same 10-step shape as the exercise plan.*

| # | Step | What you do | When | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Access** | IPUMS MEPS account; download prescribed medicines, office visits, full-year files | Nov | Data opens |
| 2 | **Read** | MEPS GLP-1 papers above; Grossman; one or two papers on drug–care substitution. Notes in `reading/` | Nov–Dec | You can say what's known and what isn't |
| 3 | **Explore** | Read codebooks; find class 373; **count new starters per panel** | Nov–Dec | The go / no-go number (Dec 1) |
| 4 | **Build** | Link prescriptions to people and visits; mark starters vs. never-starters; one row per person per period | Dec | One tidy file |
| 5 | **Describe** | Visits, tests, spending before vs. after for starters vs. others | Dec | The one striking chart |
| 6 | **Analyze** | Difference-in-differences / event study; splits | Dec–Jan | A results table in one sentence |
| 7 | **Check** | Pool panels; different outcome measures; drop 2020 | Jan | Results hold |
| 8 | **Write** | 10–15 pages | Jan | Full draft |
| 9 | **Feedback** | Health economist reads it | Jan–Feb | Second draft |
| 10 | **Share** | Post, content, email cited authors, FIU conference | Feb–Apr | Public |

**Hardest parts:** linking MEPS files (event files to people) and the small number
of new starters. **Videos and reading:** same as `../glp1-exercise/process.md`
(Angrist series; Pischke).

**Fallback if too few new starters:** compare GLP-1 users with similar non-users in
one year (weaker, but still a paper), or move to the exercise paper with UAS.
