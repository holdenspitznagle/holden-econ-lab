# Trade business consolidation (South Florida HVAC)

**Source idea:** Holden's project doc "Economics Research Project – Trade Business
Consolidation." PE firms are buying up small HVAC, plumbing and electrical
businesses. What does that mean for owners, competition and customers? Focus:
Miami-Dade first.

**Role:** paper for the spring internship (Miami HVAC company, operations and
growth). Main focus once the internship starts.

## Narrowed questions (doable for a first paper)
- **Q1, ownership only:** map PE-backed HVAC ownership in Miami-Dade and measure
  concentration by area. Skip prices (mostly not public). Hard part: PE platforms
  often keep local brand names, so ownership takes detective work.
- **Q3, valuation gap:** asking multiples on small-business listings vs. reported
  platform multiples.
- **Q2, why owners close instead of selling:** save for internship interviews.
  Verify the "92% of exits were closures" figure against the Olin report first.

## Public data (can start now)
PE deal trackers (PrivSource, Dealseam, Mergr) · Florida DBPR licenses · county
permits (BuildZoom) · business-for-sale listings (BizBuySell, BizQuest).

## Now: the listings habit
About 20 minutes a week, add South Florida HVAC business-for-sale listings to
`listings.csv`. Listings disappear once businesses sell, so a log started now is
data nobody else has by spring.
