# What is a trades business worth, and why do so many never sell?

*Reshaped 2026-11-05 from the Miami PE-consolidation idea. National, no interviews.*

**Why reshaped:** Holden transfers out of Miami for fall 2027, and a Miami-only,
PE-only question matters to fewer people. This version applies to any HVAC,
plumbing or electrical business anywhere, and can be done online.

## The question
Buyers pay far more for businesses that run without the owner and have recurring
revenue. Most small trades businesses have neither, so they sell cheaply or not at
all. **What drives a trades business's value, and how big is the gap between a
typical shop and what buyers pay most for?**

That gap explains two things at once:
- **Why so many close instead of selling** (the exit problem).
- **Why PE roll-ups make money:** buy at a low multiple, add systems and recurring
  revenue, sell at a high one ("multiple arbitrage"). PE is one buyer type, the context.

## Data
- **Main:** national business-for-sale listings for HVAC, plumbing and electrical
  (BizBuySell, BizQuest and similar), collected by hand into `listings.csv`.
  Check each site's terms of use; no scraping tools.
- **Context:** published owner-exit reports (verify every number first), PE deal
  trackers for platform multiples, Totter (SSRN) for the national PE cycle.
- **No interviews.** The Miami internship gives context and ideas, not data.

## Key limits (say them up front)
- **Asking prices are not sale prices.** Owners and brokers set them; actual deals
  may close lower.
- **Listed businesses aren't all businesses.** The ones that close never appear.
- Results are "linked to," not "caused by."
